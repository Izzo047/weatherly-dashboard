# Weatherly Code of Conduct

Weatherly is a community project, and every useful contribution starts with a respectful conversation. We welcome people of all backgrounds and experience levels, whether you are fixing a typo, improving the interface, testing a forecast, or proposing a larger idea.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that help build a healthy community include:

- Being kind, patient, and constructive.
- Assuming good intent while staying open to feedback.
- Explaining technical decisions clearly and focusing feedback on the work.
- Welcoming beginner questions and making room for different perspectives.
- Respecting people's time, privacy, identity, and boundaries.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualised or violent content, unwanted contact, trolling, and any conduct that would make a reasonable person feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Where this applies

This Code of Conduct applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where someone is representing Weatherly or its community.

## Reporting a concern

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please contact the project maintainers privately through GitHub. Do not open a public issue for a sensitive report. Include what happened, where it happened, and any context or links that can help us respond.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. The maintainers will review the situation, speak with the people involved when appropriate, and take action that may include a warning, content removal, or restriction from community spaces.

## Good-faith participation

We expect reports to be made honestly and responsibly. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern or participates in a review is not acceptable.

Thank you for helping make Weatherly a place where people can learn, experiment, and contribute with confidence.
